Data Deletion

Effective November 16, 2026

We don't keep your data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Everything Birit Box saves is on your own device, and you can remove it yourself in a minute.

Remove your Facebook connection

  1. In the Birit Box Windows app, open Go Live → Facebook → Facebook Login and press Disconnect. This deletes the Facebook sign-in from your PC.
  2. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find Birit Box and press Remove. This takes back the permissions you gave.

Remove your YouTube connection

  1. In the Birit Box Windows app, open Go Live → YouTube → YouTube Login and press Disconnect.
  2. In your Google Account, go to Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services (myaccount.google.com/connections), choose Birit Box and delete the connection.

Delete everything Birit Box saved

Android

Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Birit Box → Storage and press Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes your name, history, recordings and settings. (Menu names can differ a little between phone brands.)

Windows

Uninstall Birit Box, or delete its folder. Your stream keys, Facebook / YouTube connection, history and recordings are stored there and on that PC only.

Lives you already streamed

Live videos you streamed stay on Facebook, YouTube or TikTok as posts or videos of your account, under that platform's rules. Delete them there if you want them gone — Birit Box has no copy.
