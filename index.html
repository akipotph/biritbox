About the app

What is Birit Box?

In Filipino, birit means to belt out a song — the high note everyone waits for. Birit Box is a videoke app made for that moment: at home, in the tambayan, or with the barkada across town. It's inspired by Song Hub and Kara Okay — maraming salamat sa inspirasyon!

This isn't your typical karaoke, though. Birit Box goes retro — warm colors, old-school stripes and a laid-back, pang-chillin' vibe.

It turns a PC or a phone into a videoke. Pick a song, the lyrics roll on screen with the music, and a score card tells you how you did. Sing alone, line up the whole group, or connect with a friend in another place for a Duet or a Tapatan.

There's nothing to sign up for. Birit Box runs on your own device, and what you sing, record and save stays there.
